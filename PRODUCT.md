## Platform

web

## Users

People who want to understand Ghandy and decide whether his contribution could be useful to them, including people outside software, infrastructure, security, or AI.

Do not require visitors to identify with a profession, industry, buyer persona, or technical category before they can understand the site. No client-research dataset has been supplied.

## Product Purpose

The primary outcome is attracting prospective clients. The whole experience must help people understand who Ghandy is, what he could contribute, and why a conversation might be worthwhile.

The site remains a personal portfolio with work, ideas, methods, and professional identity. It is not only a sales landing page, a technology inventory, or a narrowly targeted CV.

## Positioning

Preserve a coherent interdisciplinary identity rather than reducing Ghandy to one job title or toolset. The existing site connects problem-solving with backend development, infrastructure, security, automation, and AI.

Present breadth through supported work and clear explanations. Do not convert interests, study, experiments, or proposed services into unsupported claims of expertise or client delivery.

## Operating Context

The current public surfaces are Home, About, Blog and its articles, Projects and their details, and a not-found page. See [the project README](README.md).

Site content is English-only for now. An English/Spanish toggle is deferred.

Existing project, article, and contact links provide the starting point. New contact mechanisms, service packages, and public claims are not established by this record.

## Brand Commitments

Use the current site identity, Ghandy. The user wants the site to communicate who they are, not only what tools they use.

Knowledge, aspiration, and complicity are user-declared relevance criteria. They are not established psychological traits of every visitor.

Selection of legacy logo variants or replacement public artwork remains a separate brand decision. This record does not establish a new visual style.

## Product Principles

1. **Knowledge:** help people understand a problem, the reasoning, and the evidence rather than confronting them with unexplained jargon.
2. **Aspiration:** make a credible better possibility imaginable without promising unproved results.
3. **Complicity:** support recognition, shared language, and trust; never assume fandom, agreement, or personal familiarity.
4. **Accessible depth:** make the entry understandable and let people explore greater detail without flattening the profile.
5. **Coherent completeness:** include what serves the experience, not everything at the same visual weight or every available feature.
